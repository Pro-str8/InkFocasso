Aftercare

Healing guide

Save this page. Come back to it instead of digging through old messages.

  1. First 24 hours

    Leave the wrap on for 24 hours, or until the following morning if your session ran late.

  2. Washing

    Wash the area 2 to 3 times a day with unscented antibacterial soap. Pat it dry, don't rub.

  3. Moisture

    Apply a thin layer of Aquaphor or A&D. Thin means you can still see the tattoo through it, not a thick coat.

  4. Healing

    Don't scratch or pick at it, even when it flakes. Let it fall off on its own.
