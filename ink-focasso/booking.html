Send the DM

Booking happens through Instagram DMs, not this site. Message @focasso_hq with these four things and you'll get a faster answer:

  1. Placement. Where on your body.
  2. Size. Roughly how much space, in inches or something like half sleeve or full sleeve.
  3. Design. Your idea and any reference images.
  4. Timing. Ask what days are open, or if there's a squeeze-in slot.
